What the app is for

My BP Numbers is a personal record-keeping tool. It helps you write down, organise and review measurements you have taken yourself, and print or share a report of them.

The app records:

  • Blood pressure
  • Pulse
  • Weight
  • Blood oxygen (SpO₂)
  • A note against any reading

It can also produce a PDF report and a spreadsheet of what you have recorded, for your own records or to give to a doctor.

You are responsible for what you enter. The app keeps the numbers you type; it does not measure anything itself and does not check whether a reading is plausible or correct.

My BP Numbers Pro — subscription and billing

Recording readings and printing a report are free, and stay free. My BP Numbers Pro is an optional yearly subscription. It adds four things:

  • Reports for months or years. The free report covers up to ten days; Pro covers any stretch of time you choose, up to your whole record.
  • Longer insights on screen. The Trend and Breakdown views reach back a month or three months, and the Averages view adds thirty-day and all-time averages; the free views cover the last week or two weeks.
  • Three daily reminders instead of one, at times you choose.
  • A spreadsheet of your readings. Save what you have recorded as a .csv file, from Settings or from the Report tab.

Everything you have recorded stays in the app and on view in History, on either tier.

Auto-renewing subscription

My BP Numbers Pro is an annual subscription at the price shown in the app, in your own currency. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase.

It renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Apple charges the renewal within the 24 hours before each period ends.

Your data, iCloud, and backups

What you record is stored on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud account, so it survives a lost or replaced phone. There is no account to create, no sign-up and no advertising, and your readings are not sent to us.

Because the store is your own iCloud, deleting the app does not delete your readings. To erase everything, use Settings → Delete everything inside the app.

With a Pro subscription, the app can also save a copy of your readings as a spreadsheet at any time. We recommend keeping your own copy of anything you would not want to lose.
